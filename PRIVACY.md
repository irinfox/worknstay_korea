# Privacy Policy

This site uses privacy-first, cookieless analytics to understand how it is used and to improve the user experience.

**What data is collected:**
* Anonymous page views and interactions (e.g., clicks on navigation, external links, calculator submissions).
* Calculator inputs are strictly generalized. Income is stored as a broad bucket (a ratio of the GNI), and age is grouped into ranges (e.g., "30–34"). Exact numbers are never saved.
* Generalized device category (mobile/desktop) and country (based on Cloudflare).

**What IS NOT collected:**
* No cookies or local storage tracking entries are placed on your device.
* No IP addresses, raw User-Agents, or personal identifiers (PII) are saved.
* No cross-site tracking or persistent individual profiles.

**How data is stored:**
* Project uses **PostHog** and **Cloudflare D1** to process and store this anonymous data.
* To ensure strict data protection standards, the PostHog instance is hosted exclusively on **EU servers**.

All analytics data is processed anonymously without tying events to individual visitors.
Full analytics pipeline is presented in [`TRACKING_PLAN.md`](./docs/TRACKING_PLAN.md).
